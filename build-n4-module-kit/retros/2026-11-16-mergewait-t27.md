## What happened
#711 decided a merge error on one state read when it matched a refusal pattern. Two cases broke the premise
that a refusal means nothing happened: a merge call sent again after a network error can be refused ("not
mergeable") because its first send merged, and the patterns `GraphQL: ` and any `HTTP 4xx` also matched a
rate limit (429, "rate limit"), a request timeout (408) and GitHub's GraphQL server error ("Something went
wrong while executing your query. This may be the result of a timeout"), none of them a verdict on the merge.
With GitHub's usual lag, either case reported exit 8 "nothing merged" for a PR that merged. Nothing could merge
a wrong head: the call keeps `--match-head-commit` and success still needs MERGED at the checked head. The
third advisory, an untested network retry of a settle read, held as a gap only: the behavior was right.
