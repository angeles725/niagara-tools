## What happened
Most of the unit's claims are slot flags and defaults read from kitControl, schedule, control and history vineflower
copies. Vineflower prints the flag argument as an integer sum (`newProperty(74, …)`, `newProperty(265, …)`), so
"TRANSIENT" or "not transient" is only a claim after the integer is decoded against `Flags.java`. Two premises fell
when the code was read: LC5's "the alarm extension is notification-only" (it owns three status bits) and the
register's "a config change starts `<name>_cfg0`" (the old records move there; the new history keeps the name).
