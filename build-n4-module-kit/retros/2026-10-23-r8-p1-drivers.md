## What happened
The 4.15.6.20 PingMonitor guide says that "in most drivers" a poll with no response makes the monitor note a ping
fail. driver-rt has no such call: `readFail` only marks the point. BACnet gets the behaviour because its own poll path
calls `ping()` and counts `maxPollTimeouts`. A custom driver that trusts the guide sentence shows a dead device as
UP with every point in fault. The hedge ("in most drivers") was the signal that the sentence describes vendor
policy, not the framework.
