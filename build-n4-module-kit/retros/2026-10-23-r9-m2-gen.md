## What happened
The three kit generators (gen-retro-index, gen-lint-index, gen-build-state) each carried the same
check-or-write flow: `[--check] [<kit-root>]` parsing, the kit sub-directory guard, the stale/missing/fresh
messages, a `showDiff` copy and the write. sweep-build-state checked two of them by building `--check` argv for
their `Main` and wrapping its stdio to drop the "is fresh" line. The flow now lives once in `go/internal/gen`:
each generator scans, reports its own input problems and renders, then hands a `gen.File` to `Run`. The two
styles differ only in data: the INDEX.md generators compare ignoring trailing newlines and report a non-regular
target as missing; gen-build-state compares exactly, notes " vs ledger.d/" and writes atomically.
`retroindex.Check(root, diag)` and `buildstate.Check(root, diag)` are the typed freshness API sweep-build-state
now calls. The over-long sweep-build-state `content`/`age` and sweep-fold-audit `Main` were split into named
steps. `lintindex.Extract` was left alone, since lintindex.go is owned by another unit.

## Lessons
- Two output styles that differ only in compare, note and write mode are data on one type, not two helpers.
- A check called in process needs a typed API; building argv for another command's `Main` hides the contract.
- A byte-identical refactor needs a captured old-vs-new matrix over error shapes, not only the happy path.
