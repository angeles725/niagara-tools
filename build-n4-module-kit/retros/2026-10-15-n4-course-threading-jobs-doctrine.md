## What happened
The user built and ran the course's Lab 9 (five executors) and Lab 10 (BSimpleJob + service) on a live station. The
course's threading doctrine contradicts the kit in two places and extends it in four. (1) The kit forbids
`new Thread`/`Executors.*` in a BComponent as "denied by the SecurityManager, throws at runtime"
(`types/logic.md:344`, `types/math-control-libraries.md:91`, `:132`, FAIL in `toolbelt/lint-timers.sh:477-485`); Tridium
teaches a named dedicated thread, an `ExecutorService` and a `BWorker` as three production-grade options, and only
`shutdownNow()` needs `RuntimePermission modifyThread`, granted by the `MANAGE_EXECUTION` permission group. The user's
module ran all three after adding `MANAGE_EXECUTION`. (2) `types/logic-authoring.md:216-233` says `BJobs.submit` posts
to a `ForkJoinPool` and must catch `RejectedExecutionException`; corpus B1238 §1238.8 (code) shows a `BSimpleJob`
runs on its own `JobThread` and never touches the pool (the kit inherited B1130's drift). The kit has no rule for the
Fox-dispatcher cost of a plain action, the `post()` override, `Invocation` vs bare `doX()`, ASYNC first-argument
coalescing, mutual exclusion, thread naming/priority, or the job cancel/heartbeat contract. None of these appear in
any kit file or retro (grep `MANAGE_EXECUTION`, `new Invocation` outside the weather example, `intrinsic lock`,
`JobCancelException`, `isAlive()`, `heartbeat()` → 0 non-retro hits, 2026-10-15).

## Lessons
- A kit "never" rule backed only by corpus inference must be re-checked against Tridium-authored training; here the course reversed a FAIL-level ban.
- The permission system, not the API, is the gate: thread creation runs; thread modification (`shutdownNow`, `interrupt`) needs `MANAGE_EXECUTION`.
- Offloading an action is two choices — which thread (dedicated, executor, worker) and how to invoke (`Invocation` keeps the action pipeline, a bare `doX()` does not).
- `BSimpleJob` owns its thread; the shared job pool is opt-in, so pool-saturation advice does not apply to it.
