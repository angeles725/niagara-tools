## What happened
Decision R8-D12 blocks every XML or `.bog` lint port until one shared scanner exists. Step 1 adds it as
`go/internal/xmlsrc` with no consumer yet, in eleven commits: a position table and `path:line:col` `*Error`,
`Open`/`Parse` over a strict `encoding/xml` tokenizer, the document checks the tokenizer leaves out (matching
end tags, one root, unique attributes), `Find`/`Children`/`Attr` and `Text` read on demand from a byte span,
`UnescapeName` for `.bog` slot names, zipped `.bog` read through `Open`, the gaps closed against expat's verdicts,
ISO-8859-1/US-ASCII decoding and an external-ID DOCTYPE. `go/README.md` gains the layout row and the
"XML rules" section the package doc already pointed at.
