## What happened
Folding the outbound-HTTP ladder (C43) exposed a wrong claim already in the kit: § 7 and gotcha H2 said a
non-driver `BHttpClient` "receives a `ServiceException`". httpClient-rt has no such class; the refusal is caught,
written to the client's health as a fault, and `sendSync()` returns an empty string. A module following the old text
would wait for an exception that never comes and read an empty payload as data. The C31 row also carried a route
(raw `POST /timeout` as a keep-alive) that the official doc does not publish; the doc's `keepAlive()` won.

## Evidence
- vineflower httpClient-rt 4.14.0.162 `com/tridium/httpClient/BHttpClientService.java:231-252` (catch → `updateHealth(BStatus.fault, …)` → `return false`), `BHttpClient.java:277,302-306`; javap of `httpClient-rt.jar` 4.15.6.20 (same message, static-init default `iconst_0`) `[ev: corpus B1346 §1346.2]`
- `grep -rln ServiceException organized/httpClient/httpClient-rt/vineflower` → no file
