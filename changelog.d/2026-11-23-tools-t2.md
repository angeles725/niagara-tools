### Added — a servlet-free request/response recorder for pure tests, and a poll-scroll advisory

- `n4kit.http` (`build-n4-module-kit/toolbelt/java/n4kit/http`): `HttpRecorder` records the status and the headers a
  handler writes (and serves the request headers it reads) behind two small interfaces, `HttpRequestView` and
  `HttpResponseSink`, with the servlet's method names and no `javax.servlet` dependency. `run-pure-test.sh` already
  puts `toolbelt/java` on the javac source path, so a pure test of StaticHandler-style Cache-Control,
  Content-Encoding and Vary logic imports it with no per-task harness. The pattern the module adopts (a pure helper over
  the two interfaces, the servlet as a thin adapter) is in `types/dashboard.md` § Static serving. Pinned by
  `tests/run-pure-test.bats` H1 and H2 (#944).
- `lint-poll-scroll-restore.sh [--strict] <ux-src-root>`: WARNs a `*.html`/`*.js` file whose poll callback clears a
  container, that shows a scroller (`overflow: auto|scroll`) and that reads or sets no scroll position. Advisory,
  one lexical file at a time (a scroller styled elsewhere, or any scroll call in the file, silences it), not in
  `report-module`; the doctrine line in `types/frontend-standard.md` § 5 no longer says the check is owed (#909).
