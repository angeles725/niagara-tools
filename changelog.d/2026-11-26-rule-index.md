### Added
- Lint index: `toolbelt/INDEX.md` gains a `Guide` column (every `types/*.md:<line>` that names the lint) and a `Doc` column (the `[CERT-doc]` / `niagara-help/` citation within 6 lines of it). `toolbelt/gen-lint-index.sh --check-guides` exits 1 listing every lint no `types/*.md` guide names, unless its header declares `# Scope: kit` (`lint-absolute-claims`, `lint-bats-errexit`, `lint-guard-pins`); it runs in CI and in `ci-local.sh --changed`. Sixteen module lints (arbitrary-ord, bql-unbounded, charset-default, clock-zero-floor, demand-scope, duplicate-helper, gradle-userhome, inert-coordination, inline-site-literal, jasmine-ux, manual-labels, se-display, service-types, status-parity, uberjar-api-conflict, wb-threading) are now named in the guide that holds their rule.
