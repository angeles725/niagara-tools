### Added — mcp-n4: station health and spy pages
- `n4_station_health` answers the station's state in one call, each section on its own: `system` (the SystemService slots: CPU usage, memory as the station reports it plus `memory_used_percent`, version, host, time), `points`, `networks` and `devices` (`total`, `ok`, a count per status flag in `by_flag`, the not-ok entries named up to `max_not_ok` with a `truncated` marker; the status is read as `status.bits`, which no locale translates), `totals` (the `n4_inventory` totals of the same rows), `capacity` (the Global Capacity counts and licence limits and the recount times of the spy metrics page) and `elapsed_ms`. A section that fails carries `error` while the others answer. `capacity` is `null` with a `capacity_note` when the spy metrics page is unreachable or holds no Global Capacity table, never a guessed count; a status the station did not answer counts as `unknown`, never as ok. It is a read tool, available without `--allow-writes`.
- `n4_spy_page` reads one diagnostics page of the station's spy tree (`GET /ord?spy:/<path>`, the pages of the Workbench Spy view) and returns it as data: `title`, `tables` (title, header, rows of cells), `links` to the child pages as paths to pass back, the `actions` the page offers (named, never invoked; their form fields are never returned) and the `text` outside the tables. It is a read tool, available without `--allow-writes`, and it only issues GET requests under the spy root: a scheme or `:`, `|`, `..`, a query string, `%`, `;`, a backslash, a control character and the known action pages (`util/gc`, `util/forceThreadDump`) are refused before any request. `status` is `ok`, `forbidden`, `not_found` or `not_html`; `truncated_by` names the caps that cut a reply. Spy pages are diagnostics whose layout differs between Niagara versions (measured on 4.15).
