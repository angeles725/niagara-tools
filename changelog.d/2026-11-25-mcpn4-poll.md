### Added

- mcp-n4: `n4_poll` runs one BQL select several times (samples 1 to 40, `interval_s` at least 1, at most 40 s in all) in one call and answers the samples, a per-cell summary (first, last, changes, min, max) and the station clock offset read from the SystemService. A lost session or an authentication failure ends the poll early. (#891)
