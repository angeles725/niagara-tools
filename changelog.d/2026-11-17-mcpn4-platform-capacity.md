### Added — mcp-n4: platform capacity and expiry

- `n4_station_health(platform=true)` adds `platform` (station-audits S13): `heap` (used and committed from `spy:/sysInfo`, the maximum from the heap pools of `spy:/util/JMX Info`, and the fraction), `cpu` and `memory` (the system section), `history_db` (histories and records summed from `spy:/localHistory`; bytes `null`, no read-only page gives the file size), `licence` (the nearest expiry of the license section, `never_expires` when nothing is dated), `certificates` (the trust store certificates of `spy:/securityInfo/Cryptography Info` with `days_left`, nearest first), `modules` (the loaded parts' count, the list with `include_modules`) and `warnings` (heap at 90% or more, licence and certificates expired or within `expiry_warn_days`, default 30). An item that cannot be read is `null` with a `*_note`. Without `platform` the reply is unchanged.
